# Plate Trainer

GeoGuessr licence-plate trainer: multiple choice, duel between two lookalikes, or typed answer, plus a study gallery.

- Wrong answer: the plate of the country you picked is shown next to the correct one, with a look-similarity score (Jaccard index of look tags).
- Mix-ups are kept in the browser (localStorage) and listed in Study, with a "Drill my mix-ups" duel round.
- Deep links: `#fr` (ISO code, lowercase) opens Study filtered on that country (or its regional map if the country has
  no plate of its own); `#r-mx`, `#r-us`, `#r-ca` open the regional explorer; `#front` the front plates map.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages
Push the contents of this folder (index.html, data/, images/) to a repo → Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main` / root.

## Free-licence plates from Wikimedia Commons
For the countries with fewer than 2 plates (GeoGuessr tiers 1-2, from `tgm-core.js`):
1. `commons-plates.cmd` (or `python tools/fetch_commons_plates.py [PL DE …] [--per 4]`): slow and cached, keeps only CC0 / public domain / CC BY / CC BY-SA files, writes `_commons/` (ignored by Git) and opens `_commons/review.html`.
2. Tick the plates to keep, "Export selection" saves `selection.json`.
3. `python tools/add_commons_plates.py selection.json`: copies them to `images/commons/` and appends them to `data/plates.js` with `credit` (author · licence) and `creditUrl` (Commons page), shown under the plate. Then fill in their tags, lookalikes and note.

## Adding plates
Drop the image in `images/` and add a line to `data/plates.js`:

```js
{"id": "any-unique-id", "img": "images/my-plate.jpg", "country": "Peru", "continent": "South America",
 "mustLearn": false, "tags": ["white-long"], "lookalikes": ["Bolivia", "Chile"], "note": "What to look for."}
```

- `country` must match a name in `data/countries.js` (add it there if missing).
- `tags` come from `TAG_LABELS` in `countries.js`; they drive the category filters and the "hard" distractors.
- `lookalikes` are always preferred as wrong answers.

## Key differences between lookalikes
`data/pairs.js` holds a short note per pair of countries, shown when you mix them up. Key = the two names sorted alphabetically, joined by `|`.

## Front plates map (Study → Front plates map, or `#front`)
Which US states, Canadian provinces and Mexican states require a front plate.
- Rules and notes: `data/frontplates.js`, edited by hand (sources in its header).
- Outlines: `data/frontmap.js`, generated by `python tools/build_frontmap.py <ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson>`
  from Natural Earth (public domain). Only needed again to change the map itself.

## Regional mode (Quiz → Game: "Mexico states" / "US states" / "Canada provinces", Study → same tabs, or `#mx` / `#us` / `#ca`)
A Street View crop is shown, you click the state on the map. After answering: reference plate of the state you
clicked vs the right one, plus other crops of the same design.
- Data: `data/regions/mx.js` (`units[CODE].designs = [{refs, crops: [{img, blur?}]}]`, CODE = ISO 3166-2 suffix).
  `blur: 1` marks crops sharp enough to read; the app blurs them. Images in `images/mx/`.
- Sources: Plonk It, "Applicably Guessable Mexico Plates Documentation" (used with permission), and @bagaboiebailey,
  "Useful Mexico License Plates 2025 Update" (designs from 2019 on, `recent: 1`; the setup offers "Recent coverage only"). Difficulty levels (`level`), Mexico City and Nuevo León from the Plonk It chart by Angelo and Linus Sköldberg; US difficulty levels and the 23 states LearnableMeta lacks from the Plonk It chart by Keaton. The quiz setup filters by difficulty; the explorer can colour the map by it. Mexico City and Nuevo León have no plate yet.
- US: `data/regions/us.js`, images in `images/us/`, from the LearnableMeta meta list "A Learnable USA – License Plates"
  (28 states). Its blurred plates are simulated from the reference plate, not real Street View crops.
- Canada: `data/regions/ca.js`, images in `images/ca/`, cut from a Canada plate guide (11 provinces and territories;
  Nunavut missing). Crops with `sim: 1` are blurred from the reference plate (dashed outline in Study).
- Vehicle type: `vehicle: "taxi" | "truck" | "bus" | "commercial" | "moto"` on a design or a crop shows a badge on the
  image before you answer. Country plates tagged `commercial` get a "Taxi / commercial" badge.
- Maps: `data/regions/<key>-map.js`, generated from Natural Earth admin-1 + populated places (big cities, labels placed
  without overlap; Alaska and Hawaii as insets):
  `python tools/build_regionmap.py <ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson> <ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson> MEX data/regions/mx-map.js mx 24 26`
  `python tools/build_regionmap.py <...> <...> USA data/regions/us-map.js us 28 34`
  `python tools/build_regionmap.py <...> <...> CAN data/regions/ca-map.js ca 16 34`
- On touch screens the first tap selects a state and a Confirm button commits the answer.
  Another country = one data file + one map file with the same key, plus two `<script>` lines in index.html.
